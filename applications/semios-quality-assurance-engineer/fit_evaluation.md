# Fit evaluation — Semios, Quality Assurance Engineer (Intermediate)

**Verdict: Medium fit — strong functional match, but a real seniority/comp
mismatch worth going in with eyes open.**

## Against preferences.yaml

- **target_roles**: matches "QA Engineer (Junior / Intermediate / Senior)" —
  in scope per the broadened target list.
- **locations**: Vancouver, BC hybrid — primary accepted location. No issue.
- **work_authorization**: N/A, Canadian-based role.
- **seniority**: posting asks for 2-4 years of experience; candidate has
  15+ years. This is the inverse of the usual "don't reject on title alone"
  case — here the concern isn't the title, it's a genuine overqualification
  and compensation gap. The posted salary band (CA$82,700–90,000) is
  consistent with an intermediate hire, not a 15+ year senior/lead profile.
  Not a deal-breaker per preferences.yaml, but flagging it plainly: a
  recruiter may see this as a flight risk or a "why is this candidate
  applying here" question, and the comp may be a step down from market rate
  for the candidate's actual experience.
- **must_haves**: both met — manual/automated testing is the core job
  function, and the role explicitly wants API-adjacent tooling (Postman)
  plus integration-style collaboration across product/dev/support.
- **nice_to_haves**: strong overlap — Selenium and Postman are named
  nice-to-haves and both appear in the candidate's existing QA Tools list.
  The posting's explicit interest in "AI tools such as Claude Code, Gemini,
  or Copilot" lines up directly with the candidate's AI-Enhanced QA
  Practices strength — this is the single best differentiator to lead with.
  Semios is a mid-size company (agtech/SaaS-adjacent), matching the
  "QA has real influence" nice-to-have, though not in the fintech/
  cybersecurity domain list.
- **deal_breakers**: none triggered.

## Recommendation

Worth applying if the candidate is comfortable with the seniority/comp
mismatch — the role's testing scope (manual, functional, exploratory,
defect management, Jira, light automation tooling, AI-assisted testing) is
a genuine, honest match for the background in profile.yaml. The cover
letter should lean into genuine interest in hands-on IC testing work rather
than downplaying experience, since the resume will show the real employment
history regardless.
